# Stage 1 — Ball-State Checks (`sonnet`)

Verification of `data/interim/ball_state.csv.gz` generated by `scripts/build_ball_state.py --data data/raw --out data/interim/ball_state.csv.gz --bye-runs 1.0`.

## 1. Output Summary & Row Accounting
- Rows written: **200,961** across **867 matches** (`200,999` raw rows minus `38` Super Over deliveries in `innings` 3 & 4 across 6 matches).
- All pre-delivery state columns (`runs_before`, `wkts_before`, `legal_before`, `balls_left`, `wkts_in_hand`, `over_idx`, `phase`, `crr`) verified leak-free (computed by subtracting current delivery outcome from cumulative post-delivery totals).
- `scripts/common.py` helpers (`balls_faced`, `legal_balls`, `bowler_runs`) asserted bit-identical against `~is_wide`, `is_legal`, and `bowler_runs` in `ball_state.csv.gz`.

## 2. Target Reconstruction & Repair Diagnostics (2nd Innings, Non-DLS)
- 2nd-innings rows: `95,234` total; `4,433` rows have `dls_flag == 1`.
- Target repair (`target = min(target_raw, inn2_total)` when chasing side won & `dls_flag == 0`) modifies **13.37%** of non-DLS 2nd-innings rows (**114 matches**).
- Non-DLS chase-outcome vs `inn2_total` anomalies:
  - Using `target_raw`: **120** anomalous matches.
  - Using `target` (repaired): **6** remaining matches (`20248`, `28962`, `29926`, `30341`, `32135`, `45512`). Of those 6, `28962` and `45512` are Super Over ties where `batting_won == 0` under team-win coding; the other 4 (`20248`, `29926`, `30341`, `32135`) have small bye/wide total over-estimation of 1–7 runs causing `inn2_total >= target` despite a defending-team win.
- **Comparison against exact `extras1_runs`**:
  - Under `build_ball_state.py --bye-runs 1.0`, reconstructed 1st-innings run-win margins match `matches.result` exactly on **83 / 303** non-DLS matches (`MAE = 2.17 runs`).
  - Summing `runs + extras1_runs + extras_2_runs` matches `matches.result` on **303 / 303** (`MAE = 0.00 runs`). Both `target_raw`, repaired `target` (primary `build_ball_state.py` baseline), and exact `extras1_runs` totals are tested in Stage 2/4 sensitivity checks.

## 3. RRR & Boundary Edge Cases
- `rrr` is populated on `95,189` of `95,234` 2nd-innings rows (missing on `45` rows where `balls_left == 0` after extra/overrun deliveries or revised-overs mismatch).
- Uncapped `rrr` ranges from `-36.0` (when `runs_needed <= 0` on 270 late-chase rows) to `930.0` (1st/final-ball spikes with 1 ball left; `99th pct = 52.0`, `1,694` rows `> 36.0`).
- **Action for Stage 2 WP modeling**: winsorise/clip `rrr` to `[0.0, 36.0]` (and `runs_needed` to `>= 0`) inside the WP feature matrix while preserving raw columns in `ball_state.csv.gz`.
