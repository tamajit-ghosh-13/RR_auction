## 2. Why `C03` + 5-Combo Confirmation (`{C01, C03, C04, C11, C12}`) Survives
1. **`LI >= 75th pct` (`C03`, `C12`, `C04`)**: The ONLY pressure tier under `bye1_repaired` where BOTH batting and bowling split-half correlations are clearly positive (`0.21–0.26` bat, `0.15–0.20` bowl) with 83–112 eligible players. Note `SE ~ 0.1` at `n ~ 85`, so `0.21–0.26` vs `0.20` differences among `C03` and `C12` are statistically indistinguishable.
2. **Why `C07`, `C09`, and `C10` fail**: `C07` shrinks eligibility to only `23` batters / `38` bowlers and has negative bowling `rho` (`-0.0263`); `C09` (`0*WAE`) goes sharply negative for bowlers (`-0.2002`); `C10` (`LI >= 95th`) collapses batting `rho` (`-0.1353`).
3. **Final Selection**: `C03_bye1_li75_anova` serves as primary, confirmed across `{C01, C03, C04, C11, C12}` where **all 10 bowlers** have `>= 3/5` Top-10 consensus and **all 10 batters** include all six `4/5` and three `3/5` consensus leaders plus `#10 Prabhsimran Singh` (`2/5` in `{C03, C12}`).
