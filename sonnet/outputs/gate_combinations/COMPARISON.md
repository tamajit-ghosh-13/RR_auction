## 2. Why `C03` + 5-Combo Confirmation (`{C01, C03, C04, C11, C12}`) Survives
1. **`LI >= 75th pct` (`C03`, `C12`, `C04`)**: The ONLY pressure tier under `bye1_repaired` where BOTH batting and bowling split-half correlations are clearly positive (`0.23–0.25` bat, `0.18–0.24` bowl) with 83–112 eligible players.
2. **Why `C07`, `C09`, and `C10` fail**: `C07` shrinks eligibility to only `23` batters / `38` bowlers; `C09` (`0*WAE`) goes negative for bowlers (`-0.0352`); `C10` (`LI >= 95th`) collapses batting `rho` to negative (`-0.1147`).
3. **Final Selection**: `C03_bye1_li75_anova` serves as primary, supported by cross-combination consensus in `outputs/gate_combinations/all_gate_combinations_master.csv`.
