Logged in `outputs/DECISIONS.md` as D1a; sensitivity is run in Stage 2/4.

   - Non-bowler dismissals: `RunOut` (600 total: 329 striker, 271 non-striker), `Retire Out` (6 total: 3 striker, 2 non-striker, 1 missing `dismissed_player_id`), `Obstracting the field` (2: 1 striker, 1 non-striker), `Obstructing the field` (1: striker).
